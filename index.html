ABOUT CHELSEA F.C.

Chelsea Football Club is an English professional football club based in Fulham, West London, England. They compete in the Premier League, the top tier of English football, and are one of the most successful clubs in English football history. In domestic football, Chelsea have won six top-flight league titles, eight FA Cups, five League Cups, four FA Community Shields and two Full Members' Cups. In international football, they have won two UEFA Champions Leagues, two UEFA Europa Leagues, two UEFA Cup Winners' Cups, two UEFA Super Cups, two FIFA Club World Cups and one UEFA Conference League. The club was founded in 1905 and named after neighbouring area Chelsea and play their home games at Stamford Bridge. Chelsea won their first major domestic trophy, the First Division championship, in 1955. They won their first Premier League title in the 2004–05 season. Chelsea won their first European trophy in 1971, lifting the Cup Winners' Cup, which they won again in 1998. They went on to win their first UEFA Champions League title in 2012 and repeated the feat in 2021. Chelsea have won the UEFA Europa League twice, in 2013 and 2019. After winning the UEFA Conference League in 2025, Chelsea became the first club to win all four main UEFA competitions, as well as all UEFA Men's competitions.[1][2] They also won the FIFA Club World Cup in 2021 and 2025, the latter being the contest's first iteration with 32 teams. Chelsea have rivalries with fellow London teams Arsenal and Tottenham Hotspur, and also with Leeds United.

Chelsea FC Academy

In 2016, the Academy was awarded Category One status following an independent audit by the Premier League.

The audit process forms part of the League’s monitoring of the Elite Player Performance Plan (EPPP), created in 2012 and designed to increase the quantity and quality of homegrown English players. The categorisation system permits the best-ranked academies more contact time with young players and ensures their development teams participate in the elite competitions at youth level. Up to 10 different factors are considered in the grading including productivity rates, training facilities, coaching, welfare and education provisions.

The Academy teams compete in an extensive games programme in the Professional Development Phase (Under-17+). These are split between the Under-18s, Under-19s (for European competition) and Under-21s.

Our Under-21 squad’s league commitments are in Premier League 2, which succeeded the Under-21 Premier League in the summer of 2016. This increased the age parameters to Under-23 and the introduction of two tiers, with promotion and relegation between the two, but later reverting to an Under-21 competition. A new format was introduced for 2023/24 with one division once again. Home games are generally played at Kingsmeadow or Stamford Bridge.

This age group also competes in the EFL Trophy, a senior Football League competition which features the 48 club sides from League One and League Two. From the summer of 2016, 16 Category One development teams were invited to participate in the competition, initially on a trial basis.

In 2020 our development squad were crowned Premier League 2 champions for the first time, after it decided that the league would be decided on a points-per-game basis due to the Covid pandemic curtailing the competition. This meant Chelsea, who remained unbeaten throughout all 18 games played, would take the title for the 2019/20 season.

When qualified for it, the Under-19s take part in the UEFA Youth League, a European competition that has a similar format to the UEFA Champions League group stage. Home games are generally played at Cobham or Kingsmeadow.

The Under-18s, also colloquially referred to as the youth team, have participated in the FA Youth Cup every season since 1952 and have lifted the trophy on nine occasions. Only Manchester United (11) have won the trophy more often.
